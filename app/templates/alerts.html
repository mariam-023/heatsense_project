🌡️ Heat Wave Response Guide
  • 💧
    Hydrate Frequently

    Drink water regularly. Aim for 3+ litres/day. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks during peak heat.

  • 🏠
    Stay Indoors 11 AM – 4 PM

    Limit sun exposure during peak solar hours. Use fans, coolers, or AC where available.

  • 👕
    Wear Light Clothing

    Choose loose, light-coloured cotton clothing. Use umbrellas or hats when outdoors.

  • 🩺
    Watch for Heat Illness

    Signs: heavy sweating, cramps, dizziness, confusion. Move to shade and call 108 immediately.
